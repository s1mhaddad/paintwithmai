Welcome, I am so glad you are here!

I am a mixed media artist, art facilitator, and the founder of Paint with Mai, where I create welcoming spaces for creativity, well-being, and connection through the art of storytelling.

My vision is simple: Creativity is not reserved for traditional artists. It is a vital pathway to well-being, confidence, human connection, and personal growth.

My Artistic Journey

My path as an artist has been an evolving conversation with different mediums. Early on, I fell in love with the freedom of loose watercolours, which led me to study more structured botanical art under David Reynolds for a couple of years. Over time, life drew me toward the rich textures of floral and landscape acrylics.

Interestingly, my background as a medical researcher gave me a unique lens for my art, equipping me with an innate curiosity to experiment with acrylic paint on gel plates and collage. Most recently, I experimented with alcohol inks and have begun combining all of these diverse techniques into layered mixed-media pieces that range from realistic to abstract.

Paint with Mai

Driven by a passion for teaching and creating community, I have spent years facilitating workshops for people of all ages seeking connection and relaxation through art. In 2026, I took this step further by formalising @paintwithmai, a dedicated space designed to help you pause, unwind, and create art in a supportive, collaborative atmosphere.

When you join a Paint with Mai workshop, whether we are exploring watercolours, acrylics, gel printing, alcohol inks, or collage, you are invited to mindfully unwind, explore colour, and share your own stories. My true goal is to empower everyone who joins to express themselves freely and step confidently into their own creative journey.

I believe that creativity belongs to everyone. Through Paint with Mai, I guide participants to mindfully unwind, express their life experiences freely, build confidence, and embark on their own unique creative journeys.
