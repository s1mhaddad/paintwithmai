How it started

I am a mixed media artist, art facilitator and the creator of Painting with Mai, where I create spaces for creativity, wellbeing and connection. I facilitate water colour, acrylics, gelliprinting, alcohol ink and collage workshops. My vision is that Creativity is not reserved for artists. It is a pathway to wellbeing, confidence, connection and personal growth.

My paintings are versatile ranging between realistic and abstract art. Earlier in my life I was focused on lose watercolours, I then trained on botanical art under David Reynolds for a couple of years. Life then led me to floral and landscape acrylics. Being a medical researcher equipped me with tools to experiment with acrylic paint on gelliprints and collages. I then fell in love with alcohol inks and have recently started to combine all the different types of media in mixed media artwork.

My passion to teaching led me to facilitating workshops for all ages seeking connection and sharing the love of art in a relaxing atmosphere. In 2026 I decided to take this a step further and formalised my @paintwithmai brand to invite the participants to pause, create art in a relaxing atmosphere. My real goal is to allow everyone who joins a paint with Mai workshop to freely express their selves and confidently start their creative journey. Participants at my workshops are invited to mindfully unwind while exploring colour and a variety of mixed media techniques.

Enjoy painting.

Mai Sarraj
