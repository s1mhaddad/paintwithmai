How it started

What began as weekend canvases sold out of a garage turned into a proper studio practice — abstract landscapes and warm, pigment-heavy still lifes, built one layer at a time.

This site is currently a showcase of that work. Original pieces, prints, and paint-and-sip studio nights are coming to the site soon — for now, reach out directly.
